\section{Detailed Metric Analysis \& Convergence Phases}

\subsection{Phase 1: Rapid Representation Learning (Epochs 1--8 $\vert$ LR = 0.10)}
During the initial 8 epochs, the model utilized a higher learning rate ($\eta = 0.10$) to rapidly adapt pretrained representations to the Nom character stroke sequence space:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Training Loss:} Steep decline from $0.3705$ (Epoch 1) to $0.2677$ (Epoch 8), corresponding to a $27.7\%$ error drop.
    \item \textbf{Accuracy Level:} Validation ExpRate climbed from $88.30\%$ up to $89.30\%$ by Epoch 4, stabilizing at an average of $\sim 89.0\%$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 2: Precision Fine-Tuning \& Peak Performance (Epochs 9--16 $\vert$ LR = 0.01)}
At Step 336,440 (end of Epoch 8), validation metric plateaus triggered an automatic learning rate decay to $\eta = 0.01$:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Immediate Accuracy Surge:} Epoch 9 instantly registered a sharp accuracy increase to \textbf{90.60\%} ($+1.7\%$ jump over Epoch 8).
    \item \textbf{Peak Checkpoint Achieved:} At \textbf{Epoch 12} (Step 504,659), the model achieved its global peak performance of \textbf{91.20\% val\_ExpRate} and validation loss of $0.1958$.
    \item \textbf{Loss Convergence:} Validation loss reached its absolute global minimum of \textbf{0.1899} at Epoch 16.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 3: Terminal Convergence \& Early Stopping (Epochs 17--20 $\vert$ LR = 0.001)}
The second plateau reduction lowered the learning rate to $\eta = 0.001$ at Step 672,880:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
    \item The training dynamics reached asymptotic convergence with both training and validation losses remaining tightly locked between $0.1895$ and $0.1908$.
    \item Consistent with the experimental design (\texttt{early\_stopping\_patience = 8}), training terminated cleanly at Epoch 20 with no signs of divergence or overfitting.
\end{itemize}
